Unlinked

Private payment links on Bitcoin Cash

https://github.com/xfajarr/unlinked

A public payment address is a window into your wallet. Post one on your profile and anyone can see what you earn, who paid you, and where the money went next.

We wanted a link you can share anywhere that shows none of that, with no privacy jargon for the user.

So we built it: a fresh stealth address for every payment, then a zero-knowledge pool on Bitcoin Cash, verified on-chain in BCH Script.

The Story of Unlinked

Benefits of Unlinked

  • One link, paid from any wallet or chain
  • WizardConnect, QR code, or USDC, ETH and SOL via NEAR Intents
  • Every payment lands on a fresh stealth address
  • Private by default
  • Payers can’t follow your money after it lands
  • Our server never learns who got paid
  • 12 words restore your account on any device

BCH Usage

Every payment is a one-time stealth address. Then Unlinked Shield moves it into our own Groth16 zero-knowledge pool, verified on BCH mainnet in pure BCH Script (2026 VM). The pool is a covenant that only accepts a valid proof.

        require(tx.outputs[0].tokenCategory == poolToken);

    }
}
